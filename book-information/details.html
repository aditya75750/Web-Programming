01 / Fiction • Adventure

The Alchemist

Paulo Coelho
1988NovelJourney

A philosophical novel about Santiago, a young shepherd who follows a recurring dream and sets out to discover a hidden treasure. Along the way, the journey becomes a lesson in courage, purpose and listening to one’s heart.

“And, when you want something, all the universe conspires in helping you to achieve it.”
Theme
Dreams, destiny & self-discovery

Why read it?
Simple storytelling with a memorable message.
02 / Dystopian • Political fiction

1984

George Orwell
1949NovelDystopia

A chilling vision of a society ruled by surveillance, propaganda and absolute political control. Winston Smith quietly questions the world around him and risks everything in search of truth and freedom.

“Big Brother is watching you.”
Theme
Power, truth & individual freedom

Why read it?
A sharp exploration of control and language.
03 / Classic • Coming-of-age

To Kill a Mockingbird

Harper Lee
1960NovelClassic

Seen through the eyes of Scout Finch, this classic follows a young girl growing up in a small Southern town while her father defends a Black man wrongly accused of a serious crime.

“You never really understand a person until you consider things from his point of view.”
Theme
Empathy, justice & prejudice

Why read it?
A moving story about seeing the world with compassion.
04 / Romance • Classic

Pride and Prejudice

Jane Austen
1813NovelRomance

Elizabeth Bennet navigates family expectations, social class and first impressions in a witty story of love and personal growth. Her spirited independence drives the novel’s enduring charm.

“There is no charm equal to tenderness of heart.”
Theme
Love, class & first impressions

Why read it?
Elegant dialogue, sharp humour and unforgettable characters.
05 / Classic • Literary fiction

The Great Gatsby

F. Scott Fitzgerald
1925NovelJazz Age

Nick Carraway enters the glittering world of Long Island and becomes fascinated by his mysterious neighbour Jay Gatsby, whose lavish life hides a longing for a lost love.

“So we beat on, boats against the current, borne back ceaselessly into the past.”
Theme
Dreams, wealth & the past

Why read it?
A compact portrait of ambition and illusion.
